# Climate Change


**Summary**: Notes on climate change, extreme weather and climate hazards such as floods.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Water Security Toolbox](https://welllabs.org/water-security-toolbox/): WELL Labs toolkit to help water programmes in India respond to growing water scarcity driven by overuse and climate change. Full note in [[Water_Resources]]. *Keywords: water scarcity, water security, India, adaptation*
- [Groundsource: A Dataset of Flood Events from News](https://zenodo.org/records/18647054): Dataset (Mayo et al., Feb 2026, CC BY 4.0) of about 2.6 million historical flood events pulled from news articles across 150+ countries. Ships as a single 667 MB Parquet file. See also [[Data]]. *Keywords: floods, dataset, news extraction, natural hazards, parquet, global*
