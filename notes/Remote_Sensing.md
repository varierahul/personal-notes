# Remote Sensing


**Summary**: Notes on satellite and aerial imagery, Earth observation data, and the tools and models built on them.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [The Technical Debt of Earth Embedding Products](https://cloudnativegeo.org/blog/2026/02/the-technical-debt-of-earth-embedding-products/): Isaac Corley (Wherobots, Feb 2026) compares 9 Earth [[Embeddings]] products (AlphaEarth, Clay, Tessera, Presto, Major TOM, etc.) and argues that mismatched grids, CRSs, formats and distribution, not model quality, block use at scale. He calls for cloud-native formats, provenance, benchmarks and quantized storage. See also [[Data]]. *Keywords: earth embeddings, geospatial foundation models, cloud-native geo, interoperability, standards*
