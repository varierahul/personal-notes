# Water Resources


**Summary**: Notes on water security, groundwater, watershed management and water monitoring.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Water Security Toolbox](https://welllabs.org/water-security-toolbox/): WELL Labs (Aug 2026) toolkit for water programmes in India, which is moving from water-stressed to water-scarce. Covers problem diagnosis, solution design and monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEL) at watershed scale. Includes field playbooks on rain gauges, well monitoring, recharge shafts, water budgeting and [[Agriculture]] irrigation.
