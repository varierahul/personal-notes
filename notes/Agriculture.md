# Agriculture


**Summary**: Notes on agriculture, including mapping farmland and crops from imagery.
**Last updated**: 2026-11-18

---

- [Water Security Toolbox](https://welllabs.org/water-security-toolbox/): WELL Labs toolkit and field playbooks for watershed-scale water security in India, including irrigation management and rainwater harvesting. Full note in [[Water_Resources]]. *Keywords: irrigation, water security, watershed, India*
- [agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): Open-source Python toolkit that maps agricultural field boundaries from satellite and aerial imagery ([[Remote_Sensing]]). It runs models such as Delineate-Anything, Fields of The World, Prithvi and SAM 2, or clusters [[Embeddings]] (AlphaEarth, TESSERA), on Landsat, Sentinel-2, HLS and NAIP data. See also [[Python]]. *Keywords: field boundaries, segmentation, satellite imagery, foundation models, python package*
